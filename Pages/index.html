Course Backup

Back up your online courses.

Course Backup is a browser extension that saves the courses, lessons, videos and files you own from your course platform to a folder on your computer or to your own Google Drive. It starts with Kajabi, with more platforms coming.

  • Pick what to save. Choose courses, categories or single lessons, and see the total size before you start.
  • Keep your folder layout. Everything is saved in tidy folders: course, category, lesson.
  • Stays with you. Files go straight from your course platform to your own folder or Drive. We run no servers and never see your content.
